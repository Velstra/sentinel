# Support

## Community (free)

- Questions and bug reports: [GitHub issues](https://github.com/Velstra/sentinel/issues)
  and discussions. Answers are best effort, without a response target.
- Documentation: the [Sentinel operator guide](https://velstra.github.io/sentinel/).
- Every feature is in the free AGPL release, and security fixes land on `main`
  first.

Before opening an issue, include `sentinel show version`, the image SHA-256 if
you run an image, the configuration with secrets removed, and what you expected
and observed.

## Paid support

Enterprise LTS subscriptions add a signed, slower update channel with per-image
compliance downloads. The Business and Fleet plans add email support on
business days in Central European Time (8x5) with a first response the next
business day and a yearly ticket allowance. There is no 24/7 support.

- Plans and prices: <https://www.velstra.cloud/pricing>
- Response times, scope and the LTS lifecycle: <https://www.velstra.cloud/support>

## Security issues

Never report a vulnerability in an issue or through support. Follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
