**Why there is a CLA.** Velstra Sentinel, Fabric, Wren and Cloud are published
under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or later
(AGPL-3.0-or-later). The agreements below let Velstra also offer the code
under other licence terms, for example to an appliance vendor that cannot
accept the AGPL. You keep the copyright in your contribution.

**Velstra's promise.** Every contribution accepted under these agreements
stays available to everyone under the AGPL-3.0-or-later (or a later version
of it) in the public repository where it was contributed. Velstra will not
withdraw that licence for code it has published.

**How to sign.** Individuals sign once by posting this comment on their first
pull request, after the CLA assistant asks:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

An entity signs the Entity agreement by sending a completed and signed copy
to TODO(owner): address for entity agreements, listing the people
authorised to contribute on its behalf. Those people then also sign as
individuals on their pull requests.
